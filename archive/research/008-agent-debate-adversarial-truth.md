# Research 008: Agent Debate Systems — Adversarial Collaboration for Truth

**Query:** Real-time AI agent debate, adversarial collaboration, constitutional AI council

## Core Concept
Multiple AI agents with distinct perspectives debate to find truth. Not consensus — dialectic. The disagreement IS the value.

## Key Systems
- **MindMesh AI** — 7 agents debate decisions in real-time, checking biases and verifying facts
- **Grok 4.2 Beta 2** — multi-agent architecture where experts cross-check facts and debate conclusions
- **AI Pluralism** — diverse AI perspectives create genuine dialectical exchange

## Principles
1. **Cognitive frameworks > personas** — give agents analytical lenses, not just roles
2. **Adversarial collaboration** — agents with competing hypotheses cooperate to stress-test ideas
3. **Constitutional AI as governance** — ethical/procedural principles that govern the debate
4. **Structured argumentation** — arguments, evidence, rebuttals — not free-form chat

## What This Means for Triumvirate
Our council/goatrodeo pattern is already a debate system. But it's weak:
- Agents are asked sequentially, not debating each other
- No structured argumentation (just "give your opinion")
- No adversarial pressure (agents tend to agree)
- No constitutional governance (no rules about what counts as evidence)

**The v2 triumvirate should implement real structured debate:**
1. Agent A makes a claim with evidence
2. Agent B challenges the weakest point
3. Agent C judges the exchange
4. Human decides based on the structured record
